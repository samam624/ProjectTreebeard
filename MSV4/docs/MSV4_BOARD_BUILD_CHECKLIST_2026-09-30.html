Watch for

  • The pad labelled DNP stays empty. It is the 100 nF on VBAT_DIV; firmware reads the divider 2 ms after switching it on and assumes no cap.
  • C10 must be the C0G 10 nF (Murata, MSV3 stock). It holds the MPPT reference, so leakage becomes charging error.
  • Nine MΩ values sit next to each other on the BQ25504 ladders. Pull them in labelled strips.
  • D1 is the MSV3 stock LED, not the footprint's part. Check its cathode mark before placing.
  • Bench power needs an SP input, at 3.0 V or less. A cell alone never starts the BQ25504.
  • SWD only through swd_flash_hex.py, which waits for 3 s of steady contact. No bare serial commands to the flasher.
