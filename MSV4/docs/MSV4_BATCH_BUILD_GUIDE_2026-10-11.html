Watch for

  • Board 1's three faults all came from too much paste on fine-pitch parts and none showed from the top: a bridge at U4 pins 8/9, an A/B short on U3, and a solder short under R15 from excess paste (not a tombstone; corrected 2026-10-23 by the maintainer). Paste thin on U3, U4, Q1 and U, and run the per-board checks in stage 07 before powering anything.
  • The pad labelled DNP stays empty on every board. It is the 100 nF on VBAT_DIV; firmware reads the divider 2 ms after switching it on and assumes no cap.
  • C10 must be the C0G 10 nF (Murata, MSV3 stock). It holds the MPPT reference, so leakage becomes charging error.
  • Nine MΩ values sit next to each other on the BQ25504 ladders, and four boards means four of each. Pull them in labelled strips and keep them apart.
  • D1 is the MSV3 stock LED, not the footprint's part. Check its cathode mark before placing the first one.
  • Bench power needs an SP input, at 3.0 V or less. A cell alone never starts the BQ25504.
  • SWD only through swd_flash_hex.py, which waits for 3 s of steady contact. No bare serial commands to the flasher. One board at a time, held by hand until it finishes.
  • Plain python runs every script here. The portable Python the older docs name does not exist on this machine.
