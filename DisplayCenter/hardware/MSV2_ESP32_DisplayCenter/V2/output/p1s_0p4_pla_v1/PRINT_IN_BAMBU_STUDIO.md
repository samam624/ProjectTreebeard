# MSV2 DisplayCenter - P1S / 0.4 mm / PLA

These projects are ready for a Bambu Lab P1S with a 0.4 mm nozzle and a
Textured PEI Plate. Open each 3MF in Bambu Studio, select the loaded PLA spool,
confirm the plate type, and print the plate as prepared. Do not auto-orient the
parts.

## Project 1: front cover and kickstand

Open `MSV2_DisplayCenter_P1S_0p4_PLA_front_and_stand_v1.3mf`.

- 0.20 mm layers, three walls, five top and bottom layers
- 20% gyroid infill
- 5 mm outer brim
- no supports
- front cover is visible-bezel-face down
- kickstand stands on its wide foot
- slicer estimate: about 1 hour 41 minutes and 18.6 g PLA

## Project 2: rear shell

Open `MSV2_DisplayCenter_P1S_0p4_PLA_rear_shell_v1.3mf`.

- same layer, wall, infill, and brim settings
- shell stands on its continuous right side wall
- build-plate-only tree supports are enabled for the internal horizontal posts
- slicer estimate: about 5 hours 13 minutes and 57.4 g PLA including support

The combined estimate is about 6 hours 54 minutes and 76.0 g PLA.

## Before printing

1. Clean the Textured PEI Plate and confirm that the installed nozzle is 0.4 mm.
2. Confirm Generic PLA, 220 C nozzle, and 55 C bed settings are appropriate for
   the actual spool. Use the filament maker's temperatures if they differ.
3. Keep the P1S top or front vented as appropriate for PLA to avoid excessive
   chamber heat during the long rear-shell print.
4. Leave the prepared orientations, brim, and support settings unchanged.

## After printing

1. Remove the outer brims. Remove tree support only from the rear shell.
2. Clear the four 2.4 mm case-screw passages and the two 2.4 mm kickstand pivot
   passages by hand if first-layer squish or stringing has reduced them.
3. Install the specified M2 heat-set inserts into the 2.9 mm diameter, 3.2 mm
   deep blind pockets. Do not bottom the iron against the visible bezel face.
4. Add approximately 0.25 mm foam or TPU pads to the controller support posts.
5. Dry-fit the PCB, USB-C plug, internal antenna, screws, and kickstand before
   final assembly. Select screw length from the actual insert engagement.

The three STL files are included for reslicing. The two supplied 3MF projects
already contain the sliced G-code generated by Bambu Studio 02.07.01.62.
