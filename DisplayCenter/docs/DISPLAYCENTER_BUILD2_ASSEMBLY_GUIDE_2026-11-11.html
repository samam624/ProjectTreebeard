Watch for

  • Start the enclosure print before you solder. The two plates take about 6 h 54 min on the P1S. Stage 10 has the details.
  • 22 Ω and 220 Ω both come four to a board, both 0805. R1 R2 R12 R15 are 22 Ω; R4 R5 R7 R11 are 220 Ω (the LED resistors). Keep the two strips apart and labelled.
  • Q1 is the P-channel DMP3098L, in the same SOT-23 as the two 2N7002s at Q2/Q3. Place it from its own bag first.
  • Four parts are the last one in stock after build one: the USB-C receptacle, USBLC6-2P6, DMP3098L and AP2112K (the LCSC order added 2 spare AP2112Ks). Drop one of the others and the build waits for an order.
  • R3 and R6 are 0603 (5.1 kΩ, USB-C CC). Everything else passive is 0805.
  • C10 stays empty (marked NC on the silkscreen, beside BOOT). TP1–TP8 are bare test pads.
  • The receiver on COM16 is the live one. Find the new board's port before uploading, and never point the upload at COM16.
  • Antenna on the U.FL before the firmware runs. The 1U module has no antenna of its own, and the firmware starts BLE and Wi-Fi at boot.
